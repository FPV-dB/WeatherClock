    Durations

    Rain radar
    ⚠ Needs a computer. The clock can't download radar images by itself. The radar only updates while a computer on the same Wi-Fi is switched on and running the Weather Clock radar program.

    The program fetches the Bureau of Meteorology radar every 10 minutes and sends it to the clock. Get the radar program (Mac) and run its installer. Australia only. To show the radar, set What to show to "Alternate clock and album" on the Clock page.

    Photos are cropped to 240×240 in your browser before uploading. GIFs are sent as they are (240×240 is ideal). To show them, pick "Album" or "Alternate" in Clock → Appearance.

    Language

    Alexa

    To have Alexa speak when a phase ends: enable the Virtual Smart Home skill, create a "URL Routine Trigger" at virtualsmarthome.xyz and paste its address here; in the Alexa app, create a routine that starts with that trigger and makes Alexa say "Pomodoro done". It also works with Voice Monkey and IFTTT (webhooks). With an Echo on the same network, say "Alexa, discover devices": the clock shows up as three lights. Pomodoro: "turn on" starts the focus session, "turn off" pauses it and, again, ends it; "set Pomodoro to 40%" focuses for 40 minutes. Break and Long break: "turn on" starts the break, "turn off" goes back to focus. Alexa app routines can use your own phrases, like "Alexa, focus time".

    Weather

    With the warning on, the clock shows "rain 15 min" when rain should start within the next hour.

    Brightness

    Device screen

    In the test, the 1st bar should be red and the background black. Red and blue swapped: change the color order. White background or negative colors: toggle "Invert colors".

    Old image marked in the background after a face stayed on for a long time? Open the adjustment, look closely and straight at the gray patterns on the clock and move the slider until they stop flickering: that is where ghosting is lowest. The default is 0.850 V.

    Date and time

    Automatic: choosing your city under Weather sets the time zone, including daylight saving. Manual examples: Adelaide ACST-9:30ACDT,M10.1.0,M4.1.0/3 · Sydney AEST-10AEDT,M10.1.0,M4.1.0/3 · Perth AWST-8

    System

    With a password, the browser asks for user admin and this password when opening the panel. Forgot it? Use recovery mode (unplug and replug the clock 3 times).

    The update notice also offers test versions, before everyone else. They may have problems; recovery mode is still available.

    User for /update: admin. Over Wi-Fi the clock only accepts Weather Clock firmware signed with its own key; to go back to the original GeekMagic firmware, use recovery mode (unplug and replug 3 times). · free RAM

    Diagnostics

    If the clock restarts by itself, the reason shows up here. Weak Wi-Fi (below −75 dBm) usually explains weather and updates failing.
